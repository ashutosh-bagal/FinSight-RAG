## Evaluation #17

- Role: public
- Question: What does Amazon's business do?
- Ground Truth: Amazon operates in three segments (North America, International, AWS), serving consumers, sellers, developers, enterprises, advertisers; runs online/physical stores, AWS cloud services, Prime subscriptions, third-party seller programs, and advertising

### Answer
Amazon is a multinational company that operates as a customer‑centric platform offering a wide range of services. Its business includes:

* **Online retail** – operating marketplaces where consumers can purchase goods and where third‑party sellers can list and fulfill products. Amazon earns fixed fees, percentages of sales, per‑unit activity fees, interest, or combinations of these for its seller programs.  
* **Fulfillment and delivery** – managing extensive North American and International fulfillment networks, co‑sourced and outsourced arrangements, digital delivery, and physical stores to provide fast, low‑cost shipping and customer service.  
* **Subscription services** – offering Amazon Prime, which provides free fast shipping, streaming of movies, series, live sports, and other member benefits.  
* **Cloud computing** – through Amazon Web Services (AWS), delivering on‑demand technology services such as compute, storage, databases, analytics, artificial‑intelligence and machine‑learning, and other enterprise solutions.  
* **Content and advertising** – producing media content and providing advertising opportunities to reach its primary customer sets, which include consumers, sellers, developers, enterprises, content creators, advertisers, and employees.  

Overall, Amazon’s business spans e‑commerce, cloud services, digital media, subscription memberships, and related logistics and technology offerings.
