## Evaluation #3

- Role: public
- Question: What are Amazon's main risk factors?
- Ground Truth: Intense competition, expansion into new products/services/regions, international operations exposure, retail demand variability, cybersecurity/data security, legal and regulatory exposure

### Answer
Amazon’s 2025 Form 10‑K lists several key risk factors that could materially affect the company’s business, reputation, results of operations, financial condition, and stock price. The main categories mentioned in the filing are:

- **Changes in interest rates** – fluctuations that could affect financing costs and overall economic conditions.  
- **Conditions or trends in the internet, other technologies (including artificial intelligence), and the industry segments in which Amazon operates** – shifts that could impact competitive positioning and growth opportunities.  
- **Quarterly variations in operating results** – normal business volatility that may affect earnings and cash flow.  
- **Fluctuations in the broader stock market and in market prices for technology‑related companies** – broader market movements that can influence Amazon’s share price.  
- **Changes in Amazon’s own financial estimates or decisions to increase or decrease future spending or investment levels** – adjustments to budgeting or capital allocation.  
- **Changes in financial estimates and recommendations by securities analysts** – analyst outlooks that can affect investor perception.  
- **Changes in capital structure, including the issuance of additional debt or equity** – financing actions that could alter leverage or ownership composition.  

Additional specific risks highlighted include:

- **Potential adverse outcomes of tax audits, investigations, or other tax controversies**, which could require additional payments and affect future tax liabilities.  
- **Risks related to government contracts and procurement regulations**, which could impact revenue from those sources.  
- **Cybersecurity risks**, with the Security Committee overseeing the Board’s response to significant incidents and the Chief Security Officer responsible for data‑protection practices.  

These factors are summarized in Item 1A of Amazon’s Annual Report on Form 10‑K.
